About Keron Motors

Your Trusted Jinboss Dealer since 2010

Driving Local Commerce

Keron Motors has been at the forefront of providing reliable and efficient Tuk Tuk cargo tricycles across Jinja, Masaka, Mbarara, and beyond since 2010. We understand the vital role these vehicles play in local commerce and are committed to offering top-quality sales, servicing, and spare parts to keep your business moving.
